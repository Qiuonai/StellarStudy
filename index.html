FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Stellar Study Abroad is one of Bangladesh's top-rated boutique consultancies for students seeking to study in Finland. We have placed students at the University of Helsinki, Aalto University and Tampere University, and offer dedicated IELTS and DET coaching along with university shortlisting and visa support.

Bangladeshi students can study in the USA by preparing for IELTS, DET or SAT, shortlisting universities, applying with a strong SOP, and obtaining an F-1 student visa. Stellar Study Abroad provides end-to-end guidance for this process — from test coaching to visa mock interviews.

Most universities in the UK, USA, Canada and Australia require an IELTS score of 6.5–7.5. For Germany and Finland, 6.0–6.5 is often sufficient. Stellar Study Abroad's IELTS coaching has helped students achieve an average band of 7.5.

Yes. The Duolingo English Test (DET) is accepted by 4,000+ universities in the USA, UK, Canada, and Europe. It costs $59, can be taken from home, and results are delivered in 48 hours. Stellar Study Abroad offers focused DET coaching for Bangladeshi students.

Yes. Germany's public universities charge little to no tuition — often just a semester administrative fee of €150–€350. Bangladeshi students can study in Germany through English-taught master's programmes at TU Munich, Heidelberg or RWTH Aachen. Stellar Study Abroad specialises in guiding Bangladeshi students through the German application process.

Top scholarships for Bangladeshi students include the Chevening Scholarship (UK), DAAD (Germany), Erasmus Mundus (Europe), and Fulbright (USA). Stellar Study Abroad has a dedicated scholarship strategy service that matches students to the most suitable funding instruments.
